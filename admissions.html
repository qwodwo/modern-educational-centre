Admission Pathways

We welcome prospective families to explore our Day School and Boarding Programme admissions pathways. Clear prerequisites, documented requirements, and straightforward application processes ensure a smooth enrollment experience.

Day School Admission

Daily tuition programme for local students. Transportation routes available. Standard school hours with extracurricular options.

  • Age range: 3 years (Preschool) to 14 years (Grade 8)
  • Application periods: Open year-round, with priority consideration for January intake
  • Required documents: Birth certificate, passport photos, previous school reports, medical records
Apply Now - Day School

Boarding Admission

Residential programme with full-care accommodation. House system supervision. Comprehensive welfare and health support services.

  • Age range: 9 years (Grade 4) to 14 years (Grade 8)
  • Application periods: January intake primary, with limited mid-year openings
  • Required documents: Birth certificate, passport photos, medical records, previous school reports, parental statement
Apply Now - Boarding

Admission Process

A simple, guided journey from first enquiry to enrollment.

  1. Enquiry & Campus Tour: Get in touch with our admissions office and visit the school
  2. Application: Submit the completed form with all required documents
  3. Assessment & Interview: Age-appropriate assessment with parents and admissions team
  4. Offer & Enrollment: Accept your offer, pay fees, and complete enrollment

Admission Policy

Every admission is considered fairly and consistently in line with our values.

  • Age & Grade Placement: Placement follows the stated age ranges for each programme, from Preschool (age 3) through Grade 8 (age 14).
  • Non-Discrimination: No applicant is refused on the basis of ethnicity, religion, gender, or family background.
  • Documentation: Birth certificate, passport photos, previous school reports, and medical records must accompany every application.
  • Assessment: Placement decisions may include an age-appropriate academic assessment and a parent interview.
  • Intakes: The main intake is in January, with limited mid-year openings reviewed on a first-come, first-served basis.
  • Fees & Refunds: Enrollment is confirmed on payment of fees; refunds follow our published fees policy.
